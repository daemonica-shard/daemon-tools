    How the forecast works

    ROAS on day N is the revenue an install has brought in by day N, divided by what it cost. Revenue only arrives on days the player shows up, so the forecast splits it into how many days players show up and what each of those days earns.

    1. Days played come from retention. Day-N retention is the share of installs that play on day N. Added up day by day, it gives the days an install has played so far. Between your measured days the curve is interpolated on log scales. Past the last one it continues as a power law, R(d) = R(L) × (d / L)−b, with b fitted on the last two doublings of age.
    2. What a day earns comes from ROAS. Each step between two ROAS readings, divided by the days played inside it, is the revenue per active player per day as a share of CPI. Install day gets its own window when D0 ROAS is known, because first sessions earn differently.
    3. Carry both forward. Every day past your last ROAS reading adds R(d) × u(d), where u starts at the newest window and follows the path you pick on the left. With a single ROAS reading and a flat path this is the classic ratio ROAS(N) = ROAS(n) × LT(N) ÷ LT(n). CPI cancels out, so you don't need it.
    4. The range crosses a steeper and a flatter retention tail (exponent ± 0.10) with every revenue path in play. It widens the further the forecast runs past your data, and it narrows as older cohorts add D30 and D60.
    LT(N)   = R(0) + R(1) + … + R(N)          R(0) = 1, install day
u       = (ROAS(n₂) − ROAS(n₁)) ÷ (LT(n₂) − LT(n₁))     per window
ROAS(N) = ROAS(n) + Σ R(d) × u(d)  for d = n+1 … N

    How an Adjust export is read

    • Each row is one install day of one campaign. A cohort counts toward day N only when its install date plus N is at least two days before the export date, because the latest day is still filling in.
    • Each later day is chained from the one before it using only cohorts that have finished both days, so a weak early week cannot bend the curve. Retention is weighted by installs, ROAS by spend.
    • A day needs at least 3 finished cohorts carrying 10% of the selection's installs (spend, for ROAS); thinner days are left out and listed under Checks.
    • Day 0 is install day, and Dn ROAS counts revenue from day 0 through day n. Adjust's ROAS includes ad and purchase revenue as the SDK reports it.
